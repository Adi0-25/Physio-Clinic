About the Clinic

Careful hands. Clear plans. Real recovery.

Dr. Sanjit Kumar Physiotherapy Clinic is a dedicated musculoskeletal and neuro-rehabilitation practice. Every patient begins with a thorough assessment so treatment is built around their specific condition, not a one-size-fits-all routine. We treat back and neck pain, sports injuries, post-surgical recovery, arthritis, and neurological conditions such as stroke and paralysis.

  • 10+ years of clinical physiotherapy experience
  • Individualised treatment & recovery timelines
  • Modern electrotherapy & manual therapy equipment
  • Home-visit rehabilitation available on request
